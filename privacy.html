Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the Snack Size Stories app (also called StoryShorts, "the app", "we") handles and how. The app is a desktop application that runs on the creator's own computer. We do not run servers that collect or store your data.

1. Information the app accesses

Only when you connect an account, and only to provide the app's features:

  • TikTok (via Login Kit and the Content Posting API): your basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) to show which account is connected; permission to upload videos you choose to your TikTok inbox as drafts; and the list of your videos with their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) to show results in your dashboard.
  • YouTube / Google (via YouTube API Services): your channel name and picture, permission to upload videos you choose, and statistics/analytics for those videos.
  • Facebook: the name of the Page you choose, permission to publish Reels you choose to that Page, and their statistics.

2. How the information is used

Solely to show which account is connected, to upload the videos you select, and to display your videos' statistics to you. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens, account names and statistics are stored only in files on your own computer (inside the app's folder). They are not sent to us or to any third party, other than the platform the information belongs to (for example, TikTok data is only exchanged with TikTok).

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Videos are only published to the accounts you connect, when you choose to upload them.

5. Retention and deletion

You can remove the app's access at any time: click Disconnect in the app's Accounts window (this deletes the stored token from your computer and revokes it with the platform where supported). You can also revoke access in the platform's own settings, for example TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, or Google Account → Security → Third-party access (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the app's folder removes all locally stored data.

6. Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your information is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact
