Snack Size Stories makes short, original narrated stories, fun facts and animations, then shares them as short videos on YouTube, TikTok and Facebook.

What the app does

Snack Size Stories (also called StoryShorts) is a personal desktop app that runs on the creator's own computer. It:

✍️ Makes videos

Turns short original stories and fun facts into narrated vertical videos with captions, music and gameplay, animated or 3D backgrounds.

📤 Shares them

With the creator's permission, sends finished videos to the creator's own YouTube, TikTok and Facebook accounts. On TikTok, videos go to the creator's inbox as drafts to review and post.

📊 Tracks results

Shows the creator view, like and comment counts for the videos they posted, in one dashboard on their computer.

How sign-in works

The creator connects each platform by signing in on that platform's official page (YouTube/Google, TikTok, Facebook). Snack Size Stories never sees passwords. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the creator's computer and can be removed at any time with Disconnect in the app.
